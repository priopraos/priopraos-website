### MODULE 01 — EFE™ ( #efe )
**Badge:** "MODULE 01"
**H2:** "The Equity Fulfilment Engine™"
**Subtitle:** "Scores every clinical posting by independently verified community need."
**Body:** "The EFE™ is the algorithmic core of the PrioraOS platform. Every clinical posting submitted to the platform is automatically scored in real time across four independently verified dimensions of community clinical need. All inputs are drawn from open government publications. No proprietary data. No self-reported information. Every score is independently verifiable against its published source."

**H3:** "The four dimensions"

**Dimension Card 1 — Deprivation Index** (red accent, 30%)
"A normalised measure of relative socioeconomic disadvantage at the smallest available geographic unit within each deployment jurisdiction. In the United Kingdom this draws on the English Indices of Deprivation published by the Ministry of Housing, Communities and Local Government. Equivalent national indices exist in every target jurisdiction and substitute directly on international deployment."

**Dimension Card 2 — Staff Shortage Index** (amber accent, 30%)
"A measure of clinical workforce shortage intensity in the area the posting serves, drawn from national workforce statistics published at sub-regional level. Areas where clinical staff per capita falls significantly below the national benchmark receive higher scores within this dimension."

**Dimension Card 3 — Patient-to-Clinician Ratio** (blue accent, 25%)
"A measure of the ratio of registered patients to available clinical staff at the individual practice or facility level. Sourced from national patient registration datasets published at practice level where available. This dimension anchors the scoring framework in real patient demand rather than aggregate area statistics."

**Dimension Card 4 — Geographic Remoteness Index** (green accent, 15%)
"A measure applying the national rural-urban or remoteness classification at the smallest available geographic unit. This dimension captures the access barriers that are particularly pronounced in rural, coastal, and isolated communities and that deprivation data alone does not fully reflect."

**Formula Box (dark blue gradient):**
"THE FORMULA"
EFE™ Score = (0.30 × Deprivation Index) + (0.30 × Staff Shortage Index)
+ (0.25 × Patient-to-Clinician Ratio) + (0.15 × Geographic Remoteness Index)

**H3:** "The grading system"

**Grade A — Critical Need** (75–100, red)
"Highest-priority posting. Presented first in all clinician-facing discovery interfaces."

**Grade B — High Need** (50–74, amber)
"Elevated need relative to national benchmarks. Presented ahead of moderate and lower-need postings."

**Grade C — Moderate Need** (35–49, blue)
"Notable community need. Presented in standard discovery order."

**Grade D — Lower Need** (0–34, slate)
"Below the threshold for priority presentation."

**Grade Note:** "Grade A postings are presented first within all clinician-facing discovery interfaces. This ranking is not configurable and cannot be overridden by pay rate, urgency, or posting recency."

**H3:** "The Specialty Need Flag"
**Blockquote:** "In addition to the composite score, the EFE™ applies a specialty-level health inequality flag. Every posting is cross-referenced against national condition prevalence data for the local authority it serves. Where the posted clinical specialty aligns with a condition disproportionately prevalent in that community relative to the national average, a Specialty Need Flag is attached to the posting record. In the United Kingdom this cross-reference uses OHID Fingertips Local Authority Health Profiles aligned with the NHS England Core20PLUS5 national health inequalities programme."
