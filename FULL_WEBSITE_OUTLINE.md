### Section: MODULE 01 — EQUITY FULFILMENT ENGINE™

#### Core Description
- Algorithmic core of PrioraOS
- Every temporary clinical posting automatically scored and ranked in real time
- Four independently verified dimensions of community clinical need
- All inputs from open government data
- Every score independently verifiable against published source
- No patient data accessed or processed

#### THE FOUR DIMENSIONS

**1. Deprivation Index (30% weight)**
- Normalised measure of relative socioeconomic disadvantage
- Smallest available geographic unit within deployment jurisdiction
- Every country publishes equivalent on international deployment
- **UK Implementation**: English Indices of Deprivation 2025 (MHCLG) at Lower Super Output Area level (33,755 areas of England)

**2. Staff Shortage Index (30% weight)**
- Measure of clinical workforce shortage intensity
- Areas where ratio of available clinical staff to registered patients falls significantly below national benchmark receive higher scores
- **UK Implementation**: NHS England GP Workforce Statistics (NHS Digital) at sub-ICB level, updated monthly

**3. Patient-to-Clinician Ratio (25% weight)**
- Ratio of registered patients to available clinical staff at individual practice/facility level
- Anchors scoring in real patient demand vs. aggregate statistics
- **UK Implementation**: Patients Registered at a GP Practice (NHS Digital) at individual practice level, updated monthly

**4. Geographic Remoteness Index (15% weight)**
- Applies national rural-urban or remoteness classification at smallest available geographic unit
- Captures access barriers in rural, coastal, isolated communities not fully reflected in deprivation data alone
- **UK Implementation**: ONS Rural-Urban Classification 2021 at Output Area level

#### THE FORMULA
```
EFE™ Score = (0.30 × Deprivation Index) + (0.30 × Staff Shortage Index) 
           + (0.25 × Patient-to-Clinician Ratio) + (0.15 × Geographic Remoteness Index)
```

#### THE SPECIALTY NEED FLAG
- Applied in addition to composite score
- Every posting cross-referenced against national condition prevalence data for local authority
- Where posted clinical specialty aligns with condition disproportionately prevalent in that community vs. national average, Specialty Need Flag attached
- Adds fifth layer of independently verified, specialty-specific evidence to ECE™ compliance record
- **UK Implementation**: OHID Fingertips Local Authority Health Profiles, aligned with NHS England Core20PLUS5

#### THE FOUR EQUITY GRADES

**Grade A — Critical Need (75–100)**
- Posting serves community in extreme clinical need across multiple dimensions
- Grade A postings surface first in all clinician-facing interfaces
- Ranking not configurable, cannot be overridden by pay rate, urgency, posting recency

**Grade B — High Need (50–74)**
- Posting serves community with significant clinical need above national average
- Across majority of scoring dimensions

**Grade C — Moderate Need (35–49)**
- Posting serves community with clinical need broadly aligned with national average
- Localised pressures in one or two dimensions

**Grade D — Lower Need (0–34)**
- Posting serves community where open government data indicates clinical need below national average
- Across majority of dimensions

#### Important Notice on EFE Scoring
- Scores and grades express assessment of community clinical need based on independently verified open government data inputs at time of scoring
- Do NOT constitute: clinical assessment, health needs analysis, regulatory determination
- All source data published under open government licence and independently verifiable

### Section: MODULE 02 — EQUITYMARK™

#### Core Concept
- First professional equity credential in healthcare
- Builds automatically from confirmed clinical activity
- Every confirmed engagement records EFE™ grade of community served
- Grade contributes weighted point value to clinician's cumulative credential
- Process requires nothing from clinician beyond completing the shift
- Clinicians now have independently verified record of equity-directed work choice
- Structured for professional portfolios, revalidation, appraisal

#### POINT ALLOCATION

**Grade A Engagement: 3 points**
- Each Grade A engagement awards 3 points toward clinician's total

**Grade B Engagement: 2 points**
- Each Grade B engagement awards 2 points toward clinician's total

**Grade C Engagement: 1 point**
- Each Grade C engagement awards 1 point toward clinician's total

**Grade D Engagement: 0 points**
- Grade D engagements recorded but carry no point value
- Neutral — no reduction/penalty

#### Purpose of Weighting
- Ensures credential accurately reflects distribution of clinical need in clinician's work pattern
- Clinician consistently working Grade A communities builds different credential from one working primarily Grade D
- Even if both completed same number of shifts, EquityMark™ points translate clinical activity into independently verifiable equity record

#### THE FOUR PROGRESSION LEVELS

**Level 01 — Registered (0 points)**
- Verified platform registration
- Professional identity confirmed
- Entry point

**Level 02 — Developing (15 points)**
- Emerging pattern of health equity contribution has begun
- Structured for CPD portfolio inclusion: Maintaining and Improving Performance

**Level 03 — Committed (50 points)**
- Sustained contribution across multiple higher-need communities
- Structured for CPD portfolio inclusion in category of reducing health inequalities

**Level 04 — Exemplary (120 points)**
- Distinguished record of health equity contribution
- Structured as supporting information for professional revalidation
- Categories: Quality improvement, clinical leadership, active reduction of health inequalities

#### THE CONTRIBUTION REPORT

**Purpose**: 
- Clinicians can download at any point
- Documents every confirmed engagement, every community served, every EFE™ grade recorded
- Cumulative EquityMark™ score

**Use Cases**:
- Supporting information for professional revalidation
- Appraisal frameworks
- Continuing professional development records
- Accessible across all jurisdictions where PrioraOS operates

**Evidence Base**:
- Independently verified
- Sourced entirely from open government data
- Platform activity records
- Provides evidence of equity-directed clinical practice

**Important Disclaimer**: 
- Contribution Report provided as independently verified supporting information
- Does NOT constitute formal qualification or regulatory certification
- Clinicians maintain full responsibility for determining suitability within professional development and revalidation processes

### Section: MODULE 03 — EQUITY COMPLIANCE ENGINE™

#### Core Concept
- Records equity-directed workforce activity as structured, independently sourced evidence
- Generates independently auditable record of provider organisations' equity-directed workforce activity
- Produces structured, verifiable evidence that health providers are required to demonstrate under statutory and regulatory obligations
- Automatically generated as consequence of equity-scored posting and confirmation process
- Does not require manual assembly or retrospective reporting

#### WHAT IT RECORDS FOR EVERY CONFIRMED ENGAGEMENT
- Date, role, and clinical specialty of the posting
- Practice or facility location at appropriate geographic unit
- EFE™ composite score and equity grade
- Individual scores for all four input dimensions with named data sources
- Specialty Need Flag status
- Confirmation from both provider and clinician with timestamps

#### REPORT FORMATS

**1. Full Activity Report**
- Complete record of all equity-scored engagement activity
- Dimension scores and grade history for reporting period
- Designed for: Annual equity reporting, care quality inspection evidence

**2. Specialty Need Summary**
- Filtered view of all engagements carrying Specialty Need Flag
- Alongside condition prevalence profiles for relevant local authority
- Designed for: Specialty-level health inequalities reporting obligations

**3. Social Value Procurement Report**
- Formatted output aligned with national procurement Social Value submission requirements
- Full data provenance from independently published government sources
- Designed for: Procurement bodies requiring equity-directed workforce evidence with traceable independent sourcing

**4. Monthly PDF Summary**
- Automatically generated digest
- Available at all subscription levels

#### REGULATORY ALIGNMENT

The ECE™ report formats are aligned to active statutory and regulatory frameworks of each deployment jurisdiction.

**In the United Kingdom:**

1. **Health and Care Act 2022**
   - Statutory duty on ICBs and NHS trusts to reduce inequalities in access to health services

2. **CQC Single Assessment Framework**
   - Equity in Access quality statement
   - Responsive and Well-Led categories

3. **Procurement Policy Note 06/20 (PPN 06/20)**
   - Minimum 10% Social Value weighting in NHS procurement scoring

4. **NHS England Core20PLUS5**
   - National health inequalities programme
   - Five clinical priority areas

**International Deployment**:
- Equivalent regulatory frameworks mapped as part of jurisdiction recalibration process before deployment proceeds

#### DATA GOVERNANCE

**Key Statement**: 
- Every data point in every ECE™ report sourced from open government datasets published under national open licensing
- No patient-identifiable data accessed or processed at any stage
- ECE™ is workforce activity recording tool

**Important Disclaimers**:
- Does NOT constitute: regulatory determination, compliance certification, legal advice on any equality obligation
- Designed to support, not replace, organisation's own compliance processes
